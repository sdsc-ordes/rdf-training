# What successful graphs share

<div class="sdsc-features">
  <div class="sdsc-feature">
    <h3>Shared identifiers</h3>
    <p>Stable IRIs others can reuse and link to, like Wikidata items and HPO terms.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sdsc-feature">
    <h3>Shared vocabularies</h3>
    <p>Reused where possible, documented where new: schema.org at Google and Zalando.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sdsc-feature">
    <h3>Checked data</h3>
    <p>Shapes and automated checks before data is used: SHACL at IKEA, Netflix and SPHN.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sdsc-feature">
    <h3>Governance</h3>
    <p>Named owners for the IRIs, the vocabulary and the quality: IKEA's ontology team, Zalando's reviewed pull requests.</p>
  </div>
</div>
