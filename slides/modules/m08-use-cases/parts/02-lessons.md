# What successful graphs share

<div class="sdsc-features">
  <div class="sdsc-feature">
    <h3>Shared identifiers</h3>
    <p>Stable IRIs others can reuse and link to.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sdsc-feature">
    <h3>Shared vocabularies</h3>
    <p>Reused where possible, documented where new.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sdsc-feature">
    <h3>Checked data</h3>
    <p>Shapes and automated checks before anything is published.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sdsc-feature">
    <h3>Governance</h3>
    <p>Someone owns the IRIs, the vocabulary and the quality.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="sdsc-takeaway">The building blocks are the same everywhere; governance and scale are what differ.</div>
