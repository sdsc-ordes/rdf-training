# Where you meet knowledge graphs

<div class="sdsc-features">
  <div class="sdsc-feature">
    <h3>Open knowledge</h3>
    <p>Wikidata gives one identifier to every place, person and food, in every language.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sdsc-feature">
    <h3>The web</h3>
    <p>Recipe sites embed schema.org data so search engines understand them.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sdsc-feature">
    <h3>Health research</h3>
    <p>Swiss hospitals share clinical data through the SPHN RDF schema.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="sdsc-feature">
    <h3>Government</h3>
    <p>LINDAS publishes Swiss federal open data as linked data.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<p class="fine">Sources: Vrandečić & Krötzsch 2014 · schema.org Recipe · Touré et al. 2023 · LINDAS</p>
