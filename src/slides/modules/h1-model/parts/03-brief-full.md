# Hands-on 1 · Model the recipe domain

1. **Alone · 5 min:** underline the nouns and verbs in the five questions
2. **Groups of 3–4 · 15 min:** list classes and properties, each with an example and where you reused it from; check [schema.org/Recipe](https://schema.org/Recipe)
3. **Optional · 5 min:** ask an LLM for a class and property table for the same questions, and compare

<div class="cq-box">
  <ol>
    <li>Which recipes take less than 30 minutes?</li>
    <li>What can I cook for a vegetarian, gluten-free guest?</li>
    <li>Which recipes use ingredients grown in Spain?</li>
    <li>Which recipes contain fish?</li>
    <li>Which cuisine has the most vegan recipes?</li>
  </ol>
</div>
